03 / Strategic Advisory

Decisions bigger than portfolios.

Some moments in a family's financial life — selling a business, structuring an estate, preparing the next generation — are strategic before they are tactical. We advise on those moments, coordinated with your counsel and accountants.

Where we advise

  • Estate structureWorking alongside your estate counsel so assets, titling, and investment structure tell the same story.
  • Liquidity eventsSelling a business or receiving a windfall changes everything. We plan the before, the during, and the decade after.
  • Multi-generational planningTrusts, education funding, and governance frameworks that pass wealth — and the responsibility that comes with it.
  • Charitable strategyDonor-advised funds, foundations, and giving structures aligned with both intent and tax position.
  • CoordinationYour CPA, attorney, and advisor at the same table — one integrated picture, not three half-overlapping ones.
We advise; we do not practice law or tax. Recommendations are made in coordination with your existing counsel and accountants, and are not a substitute for legal or tax advice.
